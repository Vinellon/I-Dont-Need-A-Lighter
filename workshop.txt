title=I Might Need A Lighter
description=[h2]Philosophy behind this mod[/h2]
description=The first iteration of this mod [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2714198296] I Don't Need A Lighter[/url] missed the balance I was aiming at when writing it. It made the Smoker trait even more a freebie point than it should. With this version I want to keep the vanilla's lighters as the best option to light a cigarette, but also provide the player with more risky and/or scarce alternate options .

description=[h1]Car lighter overhaul : [/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Car lighters are no longer guaranteed to be found in every car. The car lighter socket can be empty or even in need of repair before becoming usable.
description=[*]Car lighter is now an object that can be carried by players, removed from cars containing one or installed in cars in need of one.
description=[*]The time it takes to warm up a car lighter is now directly tied to the car battery durability and charge level.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Stove smoking overhaul :[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Lighting a cigarette on a stove top now comes with a risk. If you accumulate too much malus(possible malus are currently coming from : Fatigue/Pain/Panic/Drunkeness/Hand Injuries) you risk having an accident when doing so.
description=[*]The time it take to light up a cigarette on a stove top is now directly tied to multiple current stat of your survivor ( Panic / Pain / Fatigue / Drunkenness / Hand injuries )
description=[/list]
